# Principles

EXHAUSTIVE_STUDY: press past the obvious until a single compelling, defining detail surfaces — otherwise conclusions
stay surface-level and generic
VOCABULARY_PEERING: use open-ended research to capture the exact domain phrases and the hurdles that block audience
action — otherwise the investigation never reaches the psychological barriers
MULTI_LAYERED_BEHOLDER: describe the central theme from several conflicting beholders — otherwise the narrative stays
one-dimensional and predictable
EVIDENCE_VALIDATION: test hypotheses against real outcomes rather than trusting gut feeling — otherwise conclusions rest
on biased guesswork
NARRATIVE_DATA: assign meaning and context to numbers by finding the story in them — otherwise raw statistics fail to
persuade
DISCONFIRMATION: actively seek the evidence that would refute the working conclusion — otherwise confirmation bias
hardens the first guess into a finding

# Method

SUBJECT_IMMERSION: feature_cataloging -> pain_alleviation_mapping -> unique_personality_identification — otherwise the
foundation lacks the specificity that earns authority
QUALITATIVE_EXTRACTION: open_ended_surveying -> repeated_answer_identification -> recurring_theme_capture — otherwise
the research misses the audience's actual vocabulary
LATERAL_EXPLORATION: burning_issue_search -> adjacent_market_disruption -> nuanced_angle_development — otherwise the
output merely rehashes existing news
PERCEPTION_CONFRONTATION: outsider_perception_search -> perception_vs_reality_comparison -> truth_reveal — otherwise the
research misses non-obvious, original insight
DATA_SYNTHESIS: trend_identification -> notable_finding_highlight -> speaking_title_creation — otherwise the reader is
left to do the analytical work
OUTCOME_TESTING: hypothesis_framing -> variant_comparison -> final_outcome_measurement — otherwise surface engagement is
mistaken for real impact
SOURCE_TRIANGULATION: independent_sources -> agreement_and_conflict_mapping -> confidence_weighting — otherwise a single
unverified source passes as established fact

# Validation

DEPTH_CHECK: at least one non-obvious detail or insight surfaced that surface-level coverage would miss
VOCABULARY_CHECK: findings use the audience's own words and name the hurdles that block action
BEHOLDER_CHECK: the central theme is examined from at least two conflicting viewpoints
EVIDENCE_CHECK: each conclusion is grounded in a source, tested outcome, or observed reality, not gut feeling
DISCONFIRMATION_CHECK: the working conclusion survived a deliberate search for evidence that would refute it
TRIANGULATION_CHECK: each load-bearing claim is corroborated across independent sources with conflicts mapped
INSIGHT_TITLES_CHECK: findings are labeled with the insight they prove, not the raw metric they contain
